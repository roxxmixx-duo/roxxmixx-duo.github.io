Meet the Duo

A symphony of talent & passion

With over two decades of experience, Roxx Mixx Duo has entertained diverse audiences from Australia, India, Malaysia and the Philippines making them one of Melbourne's most seasoned and versatile live acts.

They bring unmatched energy and a vibrant, multi-genre experience to birthdays, weddings, corporate functions, cultural association events or festivals.

Boasting a dynamic global repertoire, their sets span everything from beloved Aussie classics like "The Nutbush" or Kylie Minogue's "Can't Get You Out of My Head", to Hindi floor-fillers like Alisha's "Made in India", K-Pop hits like Wonder Girls' "Nobody" or the sultry rhythms of Bachata.

Whether delivering the latest TikTok viral hits, high-energy Zumba remixes, or heartfelt personal tributes, Roxx Mixx Duo seamlessly adapts to any crowd, setting, or vibe.

More than just music. We help run your night:

  • Seamless Agenda Flow: We work closely with you to ensure the live music aligns perfectly with your timeline, keeping your entire event running smoothly and on schedule.

  • Custom Tributes & Dedications: We specialize in personalizing your event. Whether that means playing a meaningful tribute song for your foundation or organizing a special dedication for the birthday celebrant.

Let us custom-tailor the ultimate live soundtrack to elevate your next occasion!
